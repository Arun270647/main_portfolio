Quick one before we start: do you think AI is coming for developer jobs? Keep your answer in your head. We'll check it at the end. 👀

## The setup

Tuesday evening, a Google Meet with 85 students, and a welcome slide calling me "Mr. Arun Vignesh" and "Sir". I'm still recovering from that.

Adsysinfo had asked me to run the orientation for their one-month internship: Full Stack Development using vibe coding. I had 60 minutes and six things to cover: what vibe coding is, vibe vs traditional, the steps (live), why it matters, the rules, and what comes next.

![The plan for the next 60 minutes](/blog/vibe-coding-orientation/agenda.png)

## The question that came first

Before we got anywhere near slide 2, the big one landed: "Will AI replace us?"

Fair question. I'd have asked it too. But I didn't want to answer it with an opinion.

## So I just built something

Live, in front of everyone, I built a landing page. I described what I wanted in plain English, the AI wrote the code, I checked it, and a few minutes later a working page was on screen.

The chat changed instantly. Less "will AI replace us", more "wait, that's it?" 🤯

And that's exactly the point where I hit pause.

## Five myths, busted

- **"It's cheating."** Handing in work you can't explain is cheating. Using the tool well is the job.
- **"You don't need to learn anything."** You learn by reading, running and questioning real code.
- **"It's only for people who can code."** Beginners often gain the most.
- **"AI will replace developers."** It replaced typing. Clear thinking and checking are in demand.
- **"If AI wrote it, it must be right."** AI can be confidently wrong. You review. Always.

![Five myths, busted](/blog/vibe-coding-orientation/myths.png)

## The one idea I wanted them to keep

> AI changes who types the code. Not who is responsible for it.

If the page breaks, you can't blame the AI. You shipped it. So the skill isn't typing anymore; it's knowing what to ask for, reading what comes back, and catching what's wrong.

## What's next

A month of building. 85 people who now know the tool, and the rule that comes with it. I'm excited to see what they ship. 🚀
