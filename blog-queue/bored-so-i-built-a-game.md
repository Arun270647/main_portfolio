Quick question: what do you do when you're completely, utterly bored?

I built a game. 🎮

## Dead bored

It was my semester holidays. No classes. No job. Nothing to do.

I was so bored that I kept asking myself one question: *what can I do? What can I do?*

Then it hit me: why not build a game?

## The game

It's called **Endless Runner**, and the idea is simple: your little runner keeps running. You jump over spikes and spinning saws and collect coins along the way. You have three hearts. Lose them all, and it's game over.

And it never ends. The only question is how far you get.

![The runner jumping over obstacles, with a score, coins and three hearts](/blog/bored-so-i-built-a-game/playing.jpg)

## A game with an attitude 😏

Since I was building it for fun, I gave it a personality. A slightly rude one.

The start screen says:

- *"You probably won't last 30 seconds."*
- *"Press SPACE (if you think you're good enough)."*
- *"⚠️ WARNING: May cause extreme rage."*
- *"💀 Not responsible for broken keyboards."*

And when you get going, it doesn't exactly cheer you on. Hit 250 metres and it says: *"250m… acceptable I guess."* Then it gets faster.

![At 250 metres the game says "acceptable I guess… it's getting harder"](/blog/bored-so-i-built-a-game/getting-harder.jpg)

## Today

It's live, and anyone can play it: [endless-gamer.vercel.app](https://endless-gamer.vercel.app/). Fair warning: it's still rude.

> Boredom is just free time that hasn't found a project yet.

## What I took away

1. **You don't need a reason to build.** This one started with "I'm bored".
2. **Fun projects teach you more than you expect.** Building a game is a crash course in timing, difficulty and keeping someone hooked.
3. **Personality makes people remember.** Nobody screenshots a plain "Game Over". They screenshot "acceptable I guess".
