Quick question: what do you wish someone had explained to you on day one of learning anything?

I had to answer that fifteen times in five weeks. 👩‍🏫

## The class

After the [vibe-coding orientation](/blog/vibe-coding-orientation), the real course began: **15 classes over 5 weeks.**

![The student class dashboard](/blog/teaching-websites-to-beginners/class-dashboard.jpg)

My students were smart and eager. But most of them didn't know what a website actually *was*. Not "how to build one". What one *is*.

## The first plan

My first plan was a normal web development course: the basics, then the tools, then the code, then a big demo day at the end.

On paper, it was a great course.

For my students, it was far too much. I could see it on their faces in the first class. Nodding, smiling, completely lost.

I'd been that student once. I didn't want anyone to feel that.

## The turn: delete, delete, delete ✂️

So I kept cutting.

- **Pictures before words.** Show a website, then name its parts. Not the other way round.
- **Short decks for one-hour classes.** If it doesn't fit in an hour, it doesn't belong in that class.
- **Homework anyone can finish.** The goal is momentum, not punishment.
- **No scary black terminal window in week 3.** It can wait.
- **Git saved for class 8**, when students actually have work worth saving.
- **No demo day.** The final project goes into the last class instead, so nobody has to perform under pressure.

## Classes that unlock on time

One more thing made a big difference: each class unlocks only when the live session starts.

Nobody runs ahead and gets confused. Nobody falls behind and gives up. Everyone starts each class on the same page, literally.

![A class stays locked until the live session starts](/blog/teaching-websites-to-beginners/locked-until-live.jpg)

> Teaching beginners isn't simplifying. It's deleting.

## What I took away

1. **What you leave out matters more than what you put in.**
2. **Watch faces, not slides.** The class tells you when the plan is wrong.
3. **Remember being the beginner.** It's the best teaching manual there is.
