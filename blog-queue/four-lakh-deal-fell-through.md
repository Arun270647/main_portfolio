Quick question: what's the closest you've come to something big, before it slipped away?

Mine was worth ₹4 lakhs. 💔

## The project

A client came to me with a big idea: a cab booking app. Not just cabs. **Every kind of ride in one place:** cabs, autos and other rides, all in one app.

We had long discussions. I explained how we could build it, step by step. We agreed on everything and I sent a full quotation.

He said okay.

The contract signing was fixed: **tomorrow morning, 10 AM.**

## The night before

That night, my phone rang. It was him.

*"Is there any way to reduce the cost?"*

I wanted this project. So I said okay, and sent a revised quotation. I took out my own profit margin completely. It covered only what it would cost to build. No profit for me at all.

## Two days later

He called again. This time it was short:

*"This project is cut off. Don't ask me anything."*

No reason. No explanation. He just moved on.

## Yes, it hurt

I won't pretend it didn't. I'd planned so much around that project: the work, the timeline, what it would mean for me. Losing it was heartbreaking.

But I didn't let it bring me down. Instead, I asked myself one question: *what is this teaching me?*

## What it taught me

**1. It isn't real until it's written.**
"Okay" on a phone call isn't a deal. A signed contract is. Until then, it's a conversation, and I don't plan my life around conversations anymore.

**2. Losing one deal isn't losing.**
One client walking away doesn't decide what you're worth. The only real loss is giving up.

**3. I needed a process, not just hope.**
So I built one:
- **A way to tell valuable clients from the rest,** before I plan my weeks around a project.
- **A way to follow up without annoying people.** Staying in touch and keeping things moving, without calling them again and again.

> A deal isn't done until it's signed. And you aren't done just because a deal is.

## What I took away

1. **Celebrate the signature, not the "okay".**
2. **Heartbreak is allowed. Quitting isn't.**
3. **Every "no" can leave you with a better process.**
